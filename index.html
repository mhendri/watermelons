What makes a good watermelon?
  • Field spot — a big creamy-yellow belly patch means it ripened on the ground. White or missing = picked too early.
  • Dull skin — a matte rind is ripe; a shiny one is underripe.
  • Round shape — rounder melons tend to be sweeter; long ovals more watery.
  • Strong stripes — high contrast between dark and light stripes.
  • Webbing — brown sugar-scarring means bees pollinated a sweet flower.
  • Size — heavy for its size; bigger blobs score a little higher.
